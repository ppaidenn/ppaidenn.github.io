Shrine of Tomi P

A virtual altar raised in honor of the elusive Tomi P: patron of dramatic entrances, suspiciously timed wisdom, and immaculate mythical presence. Pilgrims gather here to admire the legend, recite the credo, and leave digital offerings in good faith.

Offer one bold opinion daily.
Question all weak lore.
Carry the sigil of Tomi P with composure.

Approved Offerings

  • A polished pair of sunglasses, symbolizing foresight beyond ordinary mortals.
  • A hand-written compliment addressed to no one in particular, but clearly meant to travel.
  • A ceremonial playlist containing only songs that imply a grand arrival.
  • A single, perfectly timed nod of respect before entering the main chamber.
TP
Witness the sanctioned sigil. Do not redraw it from memory unless your resolve is stable.

Lore

Tomi P is said to appear whenever a room lacks direction but has excellent potential. Sightings are usually followed by stronger posture, better timing, and a mild sense that everyone should have prepared more thoroughly.

"Presence first. Explanation later."

Ritual

Stand still for three breaths, straighten the shoulders, and declare one ambitious plan out loud. If the plan sounds impossible but oddly correct, the shrine recognizes your sincerity.

Pilgrims are advised to remain composed while the atmosphere intensifies.

Blessing

Those who visit the shrine may leave with sharper instincts, slightly improved confidence, and a temporary resistance to mediocre ideas. Results are spiritual, theatrical, and entirely unverified.

Return whenever morale needs reinforcement.
