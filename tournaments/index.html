What this page does

Connect your Spotify account, paste a playlist link, and we’ll load the tracks, fetch their Spotify popularity scores, then seed the highest-ranked songs into a bracket.

You can use public playlists or playlists you personally have access to. For private playlists, you’ll need to authorize Spotify access with playlist-read scopes.

To make this work, create a Spotify app and add https://paiden.com/tournaments/ as a redirect URI in the Spotify developer dashboard.
