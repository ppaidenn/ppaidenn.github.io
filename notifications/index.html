Mobile Setup & Post Alerts

Install the site to your home screen, then enable notifications to get alerted when a new blog post is published.

  1. Open this site in Safari on iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button, then tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Launch the installed app from your home screen.
  4. Tap Enable Post Notifications below and allow notifications.
