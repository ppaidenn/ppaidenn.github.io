App + PWA Setup Update
Created: March 2, 2026

paiden.com now supports a home-screen PWA experience with push notifications for new blog posts.

Apple (iPhone) Setup

  1. Open paiden.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Launch the installed app from your home screen.

Android Setup

  1. Open paiden.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu icon in the browser.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Launch the installed app from your app drawer or home screen.

Notification Settings

Use the Notification Setup (Notify Me) button on the homepage to enable or disable post notifications at any time.
