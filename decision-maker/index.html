Overview

Turn a messy call into a clear recommendation you can actually inspect.

This page helps turn a complicated choice into a structured AHP-style decision model. Frameworks like this are used in high-stakes planning, including NASA-related decision work, and the goal here is simple: define what matters, weight it, rate the action, and see what truly drives the recommendation.

Structured enough to surface tradeoffs Each criterion gets visible weight, so the final outcome cannot hide what mattered most.
Plain-English questions during scoring The rating flow can turn each criterion into a cleaner question instead of making people rate vague labels.
Local session, human confirmation Refreshing clears the session, and even AI-generated drafts still move through setup, ratings, review, and results in order.
Skip the walkthrough if you already know whether you want AI help or a blank manual start.

How to read this If one criterion is 20% and another is 10%, the first one counts twice as much in the final result.

START PATH

Pick how you want to build the decision.

Both paths end inside the same decision flow. The only difference is whether you want another AI to draft the first pass for you or whether you want to start from a blank model.

AI ASSIST

Give another AI a strict job, then bring the answer back here.

This page does not call AI itself. Instead, it gives you a precise prompt another AI can use to draft the decision title, do and don't labels, criteria, rating questions, weights, notes, and starter ratings in a format this page can read.

1

Tell the story exactly how you would explain it to a person.

Include the choice, the context, the timing, the tradeoffs, the people involved, your uncertainties, and what feels high-stakes.

2

Copy the exact prompt into your AI tool.

The prompt tells the AI when to use the 2-option model versus the 3+ option model, how to choose criteria, and how to avoid breaking the import format.

If your AI accepts file uploads, attach the matching template: use the 2-option file for binary decisions or the 3+ option file for multi-option decisions.
3

Paste or upload the AI output and continue into the normal tool.

If your AI gives you a downloadable TSV or JSON file, upload it here. If it returns raw machine-readable text, paste that instead. After import, the decision tool automatically stays in the 2-option flow or opens the 3+ option flow with everything prefilled so you can confirm it step by step.
