Decision Maker

Turn a fuzzy do-or-don't choice into a structured recommendation. Set what matters, split the weights to 100%, rate how strongly each criterion supports the action, and see what actually drove the result.

START

Make a weighted decision without losing the human reasoning behind it.

This page walks through one do-or-don't decision. You name the action, choose the criteria that matter, assign them weights that total 100%, and then rate only the "do" side from 1 to 9. The page automatically treats the "don't" side as the inverse, just like the workbook logic you modeled this after.

1. Build the criteria

List the factors that matter and give each one a share of the total weight.

2. Rate the action

For each criterion, rate how strongly it supports doing the thing on a 1-9 scale.

3. Review the tradeoffs

Confirm the inputs on one screen, then see the weighted recommendation and biggest drivers.

Scoring logic

A rating of 1 gives that criterion entirely to the "don't" side. A rating of 5 splits it evenly. A rating of 9 gives it entirely to the "do" side.

Built for more than two options later
